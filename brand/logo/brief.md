# SAYA logo: brief

**Name:** SAYA. In Kazakh, *Сая* means shade (as in *саябақ*, park).
**What it is:** a route planner for Astana's hot summers. A person with disabilities gets one barrier-free route to the door. SAYA decides with A\*, Bayes and an MDP whether a shadier detour is worth it today.
**Audience:** the AI Fundamentals defense jury and portfolio visitors (recruiters, engineers). Behind them are the end users: wheelchair users, older people and anyone walking in +35 °C.
**Adjectives:** calm, protective, precise, cool, local.
**Competitors and neighbours:** 2GIS, Google Maps, Yandex Maps, Wheelmap, Citymapper.
**Where it must work:** favicon at 16 px, the landing page header (Unbounded, IBM Plex), the GitHub README and social card, the app header and phone sheet, light and dark backgrounds, one colour.
**Existing equity:** only a placeholder favicon, a half-filled circle. No equity to keep.

## Assumptions (not asked)
- A student project, so the logo needs to be a clear idea that is easy to build, not a corporate system.
- English and Latin script first. Cyrillic СAYA is a possible variant later.
- Colour is decided after a direction is picked. The concepts are judged in black.

## Category clichés (off limits unless given a fresh form)
Map pin teardrop, compass rose, squiggly route with end dots, folded map, globe, sun with rays, thermometer, the wheelchair pictogram, a tree or leaf for shade, an umbrella.

## Word map → intersections
- shade: cast shadow, canopy, hatching, cool, relief, the dark side of a ball
- sun: disc, arc across the sky, azimuth, heat
- route: line, fork, detour, dashed "direct way", door
- accessible: smooth, no steps, ramp, round (no sharp corners)
- Astana: Nur Alem sphere, Baiterek, steppe light
- **Intersections:** fork = decision (MDP) · detour = route bending away from the sun · hatched dark side = shade as the page already draws it (Hairline figures)

## Longlist (one line each)
1. **Fork** — the Y of SAYA is the route fork: the shady branch is solid and the direct way is dashed, as on the app's map.
2. **Lit sphere** — a ball lit from the upper left, its shade side drawn in hatching (Nur Alem and the page's shadows).
3. **Detour S** — the S is a route that bends around the sun.
4. Polarity line — a route that turns from black to white as it enters a pool of shade. *Dropped: a circle with a line through it reads as a no-entry sign.*
5. Sun pin — a map pin whose head is the sun and whose point is the shadow. *Dropped: a pin with a hole is the category's default.*
6. Shade line wordmark — the letters are thin above a horizontal shade line and bold below it. *Dropped: the thin half disappears at small sizes.*
7. Baiterek Y — a Y holding the sun between its arms. *Dropped: it holds the sun up instead of giving shade, and it reads as a wine glass.*
8. Arcade — a row of arches, a covered walkway. *Dropped: it reads as "m", and as a store or awning icon.*
9. Grid route cut out of the sun — *Dropped: right-angle turns read as stairs, which are a barrier in this app.*
10. Long shadow block — a building plus its 45° shadow. *Dropped: the "long shadow" flat-design trend from 2013.*

The three built: **1 Fork** (letterform + wordmark), **2 Lit sphere** (abstract), **3 Detour S** (letterform).
